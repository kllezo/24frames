**Founder & Creative Lead:** Phani Gogireddy  
**Primary Market:** Hyderabad, Telangana, India; destination and broader commercial audiences  
**Document Status:** Approved Baseline for Implementation  
**Version:** 1.0

# 1. Executive Summary

24 Frames Photography requires a premium, cinematic digital presence that communicates the quality of its photographic work, its experience in South Indian weddings and events, the creative identity of its founder, and the credibility it has established through assignments, partnerships and recognition.

The website must do more than display photographs. It should make the visitor experience the photographer's visual world, understand the range of work, discover the stories behind selected projects, and initiate an inquiry.

The central design direction combines:

- Luxury editorial art direction.
- Cinematic photography and film.
- An interactive, living photographic composition.
- Carefully curated project storytelling.
- A visually engaging recognition archive.
- Clear, elegant inquiry journeys.

The experience should be distinctive enough to stand out among photography portfolios while preserving the photography as the primary product.

## Core proposition

**A cinematic portfolio that turns photographs into an experience.**

The proposed creative direction explores the relationship between a single frame, a collection of frames and the stories that connect them.

The current working concept is *The Living Frame*. The final brand tagline and opening statement remain subject to approval.

# 2. Business Background

The supplied research dossier identifies 24 Frames as a Hyderabad-based photography and cinematic wedding-film business led by Phani Gogireddy, with roots dating to 2012.

Its documented and reported work includes:

- Wedding photography and cinematic wedding films.
- Candid and traditional photography.
- Pre-wedding films and wedding highlights.
- Event and celebration coverage.
- Selected celebrity and high-profile event assignments.
- Photography-related brand partnerships and media features.
- Luxury event scenography through the associated Luxe Events and Alankara Event Decor operations.

The research also describes a 6,000-square-foot studio in Jubilee Hills and an established presence in the regional wedding market.

These details provide context for the website's positioning. They must not automatically become published marketing claims: credentials, dates, ratings, affiliations and commercial relationships must be confirmed with the client before launch.

## Strategic positioning

Present 24 Frames as an established, premium, cinematic photography and film studio with a distinctive connection to South Indian wedding traditions, large-scale celebrations and carefully crafted visual storytelling.

The website should communicate grandeur through imagery, craft and confidence—not through unsupported superlatives.

# 3. Objectives

## Primary objectives

1. Establish a distinctive and credible premium brand presence.
2. Showcase the strongest photographic and film work.
3. Make the breadth of the portfolio easy to explore.
4. Demonstrate credibility through verified recognition, publications and assignments.
5. Turn qualified visitors into wedding, private and commercial inquiries.
6. Present the founder's artistic perspective and the stories behind the work.
7. Create a memorable interactive experience that supports, rather than distracts from, the photography.

## Success measures

Performance should be evaluated through:

- Qualified inquiry submissions.
- Inquiry conversion rate.
- Visits to portfolio project pages.
- Engagement with selected projects and films.
- Clicks on recognition and publication links.
- Completion rate of inquiry forms.
- Mobile performance and usability.
- Image loading performance and accessibility.

Numeric targets should be established after baseline traffic and analytics requirements are confirmed.

# 4. Target Audiences

## A. Wedding and private clients

Couples, families and destination-wedding clients looking for experienced photography, cinematic films and premium documentation of important occasions.

Their questions include:

- Does this team understand the scale and cultural context of our wedding?
- Can we see work similar to our event?
- What is the quality and consistency of the final output?
- How do we discuss dates, locations and requirements?

## B. Commercial and editorial clients

Brands, agencies, publications, event organizers and other professional clients seeking photography, film or commissioned visual work.

Their questions include:

- What kinds of assignments has the team completed?
- Can the team execute work at the required scale?
- What is the visual style?
- How can we submit a brief and discuss a commission?

## C. High-profile and referral-driven visitors

People who discover the studio through public figures, notable assignments, partnerships, social media, recommendations or press.

They need a concise route from a recognizable piece of work or credential to the relevant project details and inquiry channel.

# 6. Information Architecture

## Primary navigation

**Work · Recognition · The Artist · Journal · Inquire**

The 24 Frames logo returns visitors to the homepage.

The navigation should remain restrained and should not expose every portfolio category as a separate top-level link.

## Public routes

### `/` — Home
The cinematic introduction, living composition, signature projects, category exploration, featured story, recognition, artist introduction and inquiry calls to action.

### `/work` — Portfolio landing page
A curated overview of the photographer's work, with access to all portfolio categories.

### `/work/signature` — Signature Collection
A tightly curated selection of the strongest work. It should prioritize quality and visual coherence over quantity.

### `/work/weddings`
Wedding stories, destination weddings and selected wedding photography projects.

### `/work/portraits`
Celebrity, personality and commissioned portrait photography.

### `/work/events-campaigns`
Major events, celebrations, brand campaigns and commercial assignments.

### `/work/fashion-editorial`
Fashion, magazine, editorial and commissioned creative work.

### `/films`
Wedding films, highlight films, pre-wedding films, event films and selected motion projects, where sufficient material is available.

### `/archive`
A broader portfolio archive with category filtering and access to individual projects.

### `/work/[project-slug]`
An individual project page with its own imagery, story, credits, relevant metadata and related work.

### `/recognition`
A combined hub containing:
- Awards and recognition.
- Press and publications.
- Notable assignments.
- Approved testimonials.

These sections can have anchors or dedicated subroutes if the amount of material warrants them.

### `/the-artist`
The founder's biography, creative philosophy, professional journey and approach to photography.

### `/journal`
A curated list of photographic stories, interviews, project features and behind-the-scenes articles.

### `/journal/[article-slug]`
An individual editorial article.

### `/inquire`
A choice between private/wedding inquiries and commercial/editorial inquiries, with appropriate forms.

### Supporting routes
- Privacy policy.
- Custom 404 page.
- Appropriate form success and error states.

# 7. Homepage Experience

The homepage is the primary creative deliverable. Its structure is designed as a sequence of visual chapters.

## Section 01 — The Opening Frame
**Purpose:** Establish the brand's identity immediately.
The first viewport should feature an extraordinary signature photograph or a short cinematic showreel.
Elements:
- 24 Frames identity.
- Minimal navigation.
- One concise positioning statement.
- A subtle scroll cue.
- Optional restrained movement within the hero visual.

Requirements:
- No forced intro or unnecessary loading screen.
- No long wait before visitors can navigate.
- Immediate access to the site's core navigation.
- A responsive mobile treatment.
- A fallback image if the video cannot play.

## Section 02 — The Living Contact Sheet
**Purpose:** Transform the opening into the site's signature interactive experience.
The user's reference image depicts a 6 × 4 grid of 24 rounded rectangular tiles on a warm ivory background. The tiles transition from deep forest green and teal through muted sage and stone to pale ivory, with subtle shadows and the 24 FRAMES logo in the final tile.
Proposed interaction sequence:
1. The initial composition appears as the 24-tile color grid.
2. Tiles begin revealing photographic images.
3. The grid gains depth and gradually separates into a living contact sheet.
4. Visitors scroll to progress through the composition.
5. Visitors can deliberately select a portfolio category.
6. The images reorganize to reveal the selected collection.
7. Selecting a project leads into its story.

## Section 03 — Signature Collection
**Purpose:** Present the strongest work without overwhelming the visitor.
Requirements:
- Approximately three to six featured projects.
- Distinctive project cover imagery.
- Intentional image sequencing.
- Project titles and categories.
- A link to view the full portfolio.
- Responsive layouts with varied image proportions.

## Section 04 — Explore the World
**Purpose:** Make the breadth of work discoverable.
Categories:
- Weddings.
- Celebrities and portraits.
- Events and campaigns.
- Fashion and editorial.
- Films.
- Complete Archive.

## Section 05 — The Frame Becomes the World
**Purpose:** Create a transition from one photograph into a full project story.
A selected signature image expands into a larger composition and transitions into the project's page.

## Section 06 — Recognition and Legacy
**Purpose:** Demonstrate credibility through genuine evidence.
The Recognition Wall can use layered publication covers, editorial clippings, awards and assignment imagery.

## Section 07 — The Artist
**Purpose:** Introduce the person and philosophy behind the work.
Content:
- Founder portrait.
- Biography.
- Artistic philosophy.
- Professional milestones, where verified.
- Link to the full biography or selected journal stories.

## Section 08 — Journal
**Purpose:** Add context and editorial depth.
Show two or three selected articles.

## Section 09 — The Invitation
**Purpose:** Convert interest into inquiries.
Two primary paths:
1. Private and wedding commissions.
2. Commercial and editorial commissions.

## Section 10 — Footer
Brand mark, navigation links, approved social links, contact details, inquiry link, privacy policy, copyright.

# 9. Design System

## Visual direction
Luxury editorial, cinematic and experimental, balanced with restraint.
Generous spacing, refined typography, harmonious colors.

## Color direction
Provisional palette:
- Warm ivory: `#F3F0E9`
- Deep forest green: `#123C34`
- Muted sage: `#A6B0A3`
- Warm stone: `#CFC5B5`
- Soft charcoal: `#181817`

## Typography
- Expressive editorial serif for display headings (e.g. Cormorant Garamond, Italiana, or Cinzel).
- Refined, highly legible sans-serif for navigation, body text, labels and forms (e.g. Inter or Plus Jakarta Sans).
