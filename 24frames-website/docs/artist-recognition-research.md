## 1. Executive Summary & Verification Methodology

A thorough investigation of the professional achievements, awards, creator partnerships, and media features of **24 Frames Photography** and its founder, **Phani Gogireddy**, was conducted across primary and secondary sources:
- Official YouTube channel archives (`@24framesphotography`) and broadcast video transcripts.
- Official Instagram accounts of `@24framesphotography_official`, `@phanigogireddy24`, and official brand partner `@nikonindiaofficial`.
- Mainstream news and media broadcasts (Times of India / ET Now, TV9 Telugu, Borok Times, TheCconnects).
- Photography trade directories, industry associations, and public event documentation.

### Core Policy: No Hallucinations or Embellishments
In strict accordance with the project directives:
- **No fake awards or titles** have been manufactured or inferred.
- **No dummy magazine covers** (e.g. Kinfolk, Cereal, Monocle, Loewe previously placed as placeholders) are permitted; all such placeholders are completely decommissioned.
- **Directory listings** (such as WedMeGood profile score) are categorized strictly as platform reviews, not juried awards.
- Every claim is assigned one of three statuses:
  - `VERIFIED`: Adequate, authentic, independently verifiable primary evidence exists.
  - `UNVERIFIED`: Claim exists in public discussion or creator bios, but primary independent proof could not be verified.
  - `EXCLUDED`: Debunked, contradictory, misleading, or inappropriate for the luxury editorial recognition section.

Only claims with status `VERIFIED` are approved for publication as confirmed achievements on the live website.
