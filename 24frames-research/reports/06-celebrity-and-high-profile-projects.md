## 1. Evidentiary Standards & Classification Rules

To prevent promotional exaggeration and maintain investigative integrity, all individuals identified in 24 Frames media are categorized into one of seven mutually exclusive classifications:

1. **`OFFICIAL PHOTOGRAPHY CREDIT`**: The brand is explicitly contracted and credited for still photography.
2. **`OFFICIAL VIDEOGRAPHY OR FILM CREDIT`**: The brand is officially credited with filming, directing, and mastering the cinematic film.
3. **`OFFICIAL EVENT-PRODUCTION CREDIT`**: The brand or its sister vertical (Luxe Events / Alankara) designed and executed physical event decor.
4. **`PUBLICLY DOCUMENTED COLLABORATION`**: A mutual professional partnership (e.g., brand ambassador, corporate ribbon cutting, keynote mentorship).
5. **`BRAND-CLAIMED ASSOCIATION`**: The studio mentions the personality, but external independent confirmation is pending.
6. **`TAGGED OR FEATURED, ROLE UNCONFIRMED`**: The celebrity was present at an event captured by 24 Frames (e.g., a guest blessing the couple), but did NOT personally hire the studio.
7. **`POSSIBLE MATCH, UNVERIFIED`**: Inconclusive evidence requiring further corroboration.
