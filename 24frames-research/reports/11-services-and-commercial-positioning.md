### Commercial Engagement & Payment Terms
1. **Advance Booking Deposit:** **50% advance payment** required at the time of contract execution to lock dates.
2. **Balance Milestone:** Balance payment due on or immediately preceding the primary event dates.
3. **Outstation Logistics:** Client is contractually responsible for all airfare/transportation, luxury hotel boarding, local travel logistics, and equipment baggage handling for destination weddings outside Hyderabad.
4. **Delivery Timeline:** Teaser reels typically delivered within 7–14 days; master albums and final edited feature films delivered within 60–90 days following client photo selections.

## 4. Bespoke Luxury & Multi-Day Wedding Economics

For high-profile multi-day celebrations (e.g., 3 to 5 events spanning Mehendi, Haldi, Sangeet, Muhurtham, and Grand Reception), 24 Frames deploys large-scale production units.

### Production Scalability Tiers
* **Standard Luxury Tier (₹4,00,000 – ₹7,00,000 total):**
  - 2-day wedding (Muhurtham + Reception)
  - 2 Candid Photographers, 2 Traditional Photographers, 2 Cinematographers, 1 Drone Pilot
  - Deliverables: 1 Teaser, 1 Full Film, 2 Luxury Albums, all raw photographs color-corrected
* **Signature High-Profile Tier (₹8,00,000 – ₹18,00,000 total):**
  - 3–4 days of ceremonies (Pellikuthuru, Mehendi, Sangeet, Wedding, Reception)

  - Multi-city or destination palace celebration (e.g., Hyderabad, Udaipur, Goa)

  - Multi-camera 8K/4K cinema pipeline, dedicated sound engineers, same-day screening on 40-foot LED arena screens, full VIP celebrity security protocols

## 6. Strategic Differentiation: The "Dual-Doorway" Revenue Model

The strategic emergence of **Luxe Events by 24 Frames** and **Alankara Event Decor** provides a massive competitive advantage over conventional photography studios:

1. **Cross-Selling Synergy:** A client booking 24 Frames for photography is immediately introduced to Luxe Events for decor, capturing both 10–15% photography spend and 40–60% decor spend of the total wedding budget.
2. **Camera-Optimized Event Architecture:** Because the event designer and cinematographer are under one roof, Luxe Events constructs mandaps, lighting grids, and floral backdrops specifically optimized for camera lenses (flawless color rendering, zero glare, perfect camera sightlines).
3. **Single Point of Accountability:** Ultra-wealthy families deal with a single trusted executive team rather than refereeing disputes between disconnected photography and decor vendors.

## 7. Recommendations for Website Commercial UX

1. **Transparent Starting Tiers, Not Cheap Prices:** Do not list itemized pricing like a commodity catalog. Use elegant phrasing: *"Bespoke wedding commissions starting at ₹1.5 Lakhs per day; custom multi-day celebrations tailored upon consultation."*
2. **Dedicated Private Consultation Funnel:** Build an intuitive, high-touch inquiry form capturing:
   - Event Dates & City / Venue
   - Number of anticipated guests
   - Desired services (Photography, Cinema, Luxe Events Decor, Complete Turnkey)
3. **VIP / Celebrity Privacy Assurance:** Feature a prominent statement guaranteeing non-disclosure agreements (NDAs) and private server media delivery for families requiring absolute discretion.
