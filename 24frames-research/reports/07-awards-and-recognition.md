## 4. Distinction Between Awards, Affiliations, and Directory Badges

To avoid deceptive marketing on the future website, the following distinctions must be observed by the creative team:

1. **Mainstream Competitive Award vs. Paid Directory Placement:**
   - The **ET Excellence Award 2025** is a recognized mainstream media industry honor organized by *The Times of India* and should be headlined as the brand’s premier award.
   - Directory badges on **WedMeGood** (e.g., *"Premium Wedding Photography in Hyderabad"*) reflect algorithm-driven customer review milestones and platform longevity (9+ years, 28 reviews, 5.0 score), NOT a jury-selected trophy.
2. **Influencer Partnership vs. Equipment Sponsorship:**
   - Phani Gogireddy is an official **Nikon Influencer / Brand Partner**. This means he is formally recognized and sponsored by the manufacturer to field-test and showcase their cinema glass and mirrorless bodies.
3. **Contest Winner vs. Commercial Client:**
   - Winning IndiGo’s *#NoFilter* contest proves raw compositional and optical skill. It should be featured in the founder's artistic credentials to emphasize that 24 Frames relies on optical mastery and lighting rather than artificial post-processing manipulation.
