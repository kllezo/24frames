## 1. Portfolio Classification Taxonomy

Every project across the 24 Frames archive is classified into distinct categories:
1. **Grand Luxury Weddings & Receptions:** Multi-camera, multi-day coverage of high-profile unions (guest counts > 1,000).
2. **Feature Wedding Films:** Emotion-driven narrative films focused on sacred rituals, family bonds, and cinematic vows (5–10 minutes).
3. **Heritage & Destination Pre-Wedding Shoots:** Concept films shot at palatial estates (e.g., Taj Falaknama Palace) or scenic outdoor landscapes.
4. **Fine-Art Bridal Editorials:** Slow-motion, intimate portraiture focusing on heirloom jewelry, couture sarees, and bridal reflections.
5. **Traditional South Indian Rites of Passage:** Dhoti ceremonies, Half Saree functions, Upanayanams, and Housewarmings.
6. **Commercial, Celebrity & Institutional Productions:** High-profile inaugurations, celebrity birthdays, and corporate collaborations.
