## 1. Executive Summary: Who is the Brand?

**24 Frames Photography** (operating prominently as **24 Frames Wedding Films**) is an elite, high-production wedding cinematography and photography brand headquartered in Jubilee Hills / Banjara Hills, Hyderabad, Telangana. Founded and helmed by first-generation entrepreneur and filmmaker **Phani Gogireddy**, the studio has established itself as one of South India's premier luxury wedding production houses.

Operating out of an expansive **6,000 sq. ft. corporate office** inaugurated by prominent Tollywood actor Manchu Manoj in July 2025, 24 Frames specializes in large-scale, multi-day Telugu, South Indian, and international destination weddings. The brand blends royal cultural traditionalism with cutting-edge cinematic filmmaking, recently deploying industry-leading **Nikon ZR Cinema Line cameras equipped with RED Color Science**.

- **Inception (2012):** Phani Gogireddy’s career in cinematography was sparked on the sets of Telugu actor Manchu Manoj’s film *Mr. Nookayya* (specifically during the filming of the celebrated song *"Oke Oka Jeevitham"*). ### F. What is the Relationship Between 24 Frames Photography and Luxe Events?
**Luxe Events by 24 Frames** (`@luxeeventsby24frames`) functions as the specialized luxury event-curation and decor vertical affiliated with the core photography brand. On July 17, 2025, during the corporate office inauguration, the brand formalized its turnkey decor arm under **Alankara Event Decor Company** in a joint launch officiated by actor Manchu Manoj. 
- *Operational Structure:* 24 Frames handles visual capture (direction, cinematography, fine-art stills), while Luxe Events / Alankara provides physical scenography, stage lighting, floral design, and venue transformation.
- *Commercial Benefit:* Enables high-net-worth clients to contract a single, integrated team for both production scenography and cinema capture, preventing aesthetic friction between lighting crews and camera operators.
