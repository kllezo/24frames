## 1. Executive Summary & Brand Identity

**Luxe Events by 24 Frames** is the dedicated bespoke event design, spatial styling, and decor curation vertical operating within the 24 Frames corporate umbrella. While **24 Frames Photography / 24 Frames Wedding Films** serves as the visual recording arm (cinematography, fine-art stills, aerial coverage), **Luxe Events** and its allied sister entity **Alankara Event Decor Company** represent the physical scenography and event production arm.

This dual-brand architecture enables the company to offer high-net-worth clients an end-to-end, integrated luxury wedding solution: from physical mandap architecture and floral installations to cinematic 4K film mastering.

## 3. Services, Capabilities & Production Scope

### A. Wedding Design & Floral Architecture
- **Royal Mandap Fabrication:** Conceptualizing multi-tiered temple-style mandaps, carved monolithic pillars, floral domes (using imported Dutch roses, orchids, and traditional South Indian marigold/jasmine garlands).
- **Thematic Haldi & Mehendi Environments:** Sunlit, bohemian, or traditional yellow-and-terracotta courtyards designed for vibrant candid photography.
- **Sangeet Stage Scenography:** High-tech concert-style LED walls, computerized truss lighting, pyrotechnic cold-fire fountains, and custom DJ performance consoles.

### B. The Crucial Synergy: Camera-Optimized Scenography
In luxury Indian weddings, one of the most common catastrophic failure points occurs when an independent decor company installs lighting that ruins the cinematography (e.g., cheap LED lights with high flicker rates, harsh overhead spotlights creating unflattering facial shadows, or colors that distort bridal silk tones).

**The 24 Frames / Luxe Events Advantage:**
Because Phani Gogireddy is a master cinematographer, Luxe Events designs physical spaces specifically for the camera:
1. **Flicker-Free Broadcast Lighting:** High-CRI (Color Rendering Index > 95) fixtures that do not band on high-speed cinema sensors.
2. **Directional Portrait Lighting:** Placing key lights and ambient fills so that the bride and groom remain gorgeously illuminated regardless of where they turn on the mandap.
3. **Camera Walkways & Angles:** Architecting mandap steps and seating arrangements so that camera operators have unobstructed, clean lines of sight without intruding on sacred rituals.
