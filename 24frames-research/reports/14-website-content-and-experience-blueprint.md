## 5. Technical & Design System Specifications

1. **Design System Tokens:**
   - *Background Dark:* `#0B0B0C` (Obsidian Velvet)
   - *Surface Dark:* `#141416` (Smoky Card Surface)
   - *Primary Accent:* `#D4AF37` (Imperial Gold)
   - *Highlight Accent:* `#E5C158` (Antique Brass)
   - *Text Primary:* `#F4F4F6` (Pure Off-White)
   - *Text Secondary:* `#A0A0A8` (Muted Slate)
2. **Typography Hierarchy:**
   - *Headings:* `Playfair Display` or `Cormorant Garamond` (Elegant high-fashion serifs)
   - *Subheadings & UI Elements:* `Outfit` or `Plus Jakarta Sans` (Crisp modern sans-serif)
   - *Body Copy:* `Inter` (Optimal legibility across mobile displays)
3. **Performance Directives:**
   - Video streaming routed via Vimeo OTT Pro or YouTube 4K iframe embeds with custom facade covers to guarantee 95+ Google Lighthouse speed scores.
   - Next-gen image formats (WebP and AVIF) with responsive `srcset` scaling.
