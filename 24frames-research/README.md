# 24 Frames Photography & Luxe Events — Complete Brand Intelligence Investigation

## Overview
This repository contains the rigorous, source-backed brand intelligence investigation on **24 Frames Photography** / **24 Frames Wedding Films**, its associated **Luxe Events by 24 Frames** brand, founder **Phani Gogireddy**, its creative team, celebrity associations, project portfolio, visual art direction, reputation, commercial positioning, and website creative blueprint.

## Architecture
- `reports/`: 15 comprehensive investigative dossiers and strategic briefs (01 through 15).
- `evidence/`: Snapshots, transcripts, raw post metadata, and media references.
- `tools/agent-reach/`: Localized research-access tool (`agent-reach` v1.6.0).
- `*.csv`: 12 structured registry databases adhering to strict data schemas.

## Verification Standards
All claims and findings adhere to the rigorous evaluation scale:
- `VERIFIED`: Directly corroborated by primary authoritative sources or independent cross-verification.
- `SUPPORTED`: Credible evidence exists, but specific operational details remain unconfirmed.
- `UNVERIFIED`: Promotional self-claim or secondary aggregation lacking corroborating proof.
- `CONFLICTING`: Contradictory evidence exists between credible sources.
- `NOT FOUND`: No adequate public evidence discovered.
- `INACCESSIBLE`: Source blocked by authentication, technical barriers, or platform degradation.
